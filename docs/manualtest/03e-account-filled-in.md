# 3e. Your account is filled in on Microsoft's page

- **Status:** To do
- **Area:** Sign-in and accounts
- **Why it's manual:** Only Microsoft's real page shows whether the account hint is used.
- **Needs:** Your AVD account (as a normal user) and a desktop to connect to. The desktop must use Entra sign-in.
- **Background:** [ADR 0026](../adr/0026-desktop-sign-in-uses-the-apps-account.md)

## Steps

1. Select Connect on a desktop that uses Entra sign-in.
2. Look at Microsoft's page when it appears.

## Expected

- Your account is already filled in or selected.
